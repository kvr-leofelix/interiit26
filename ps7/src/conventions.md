\# Coordinate and Motor Conventions



Coordinate system:

\- +X: forward

\- +Y: left

\- +Z: upward



Rotations:

\- Roll: rotation about X

\- Pitch: rotation about Y

\- Yaw: rotation about Z



Motor numbering:

\- M1: front-left

\- M2: front-right

\- M3: rear-right

\- M4: rear-left



Rotor directions:

\- M1: CCW

\- M2: CW

\- M3: CCW

\- M4: CW



These coordinate, numbering, and rotor-direction conventions are

not specified by the problem statement. They are defined here so

that all equations and simulations use one consistent convention.
